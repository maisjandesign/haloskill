# Writing methods

Corey Haines `copywriting` is the primary page-writing method; `copy-editing` contributes its seven review passes. `product-marketing` supplies audience, offer, alternatives, differentiation, proof and objections in the shared project context. Brand-building `brand-voice` adds voice traits with “sounds like / does not sound like” examples and situational tone; `brand-messaging` adds core promise, supporting messages and reasons to believe. Miki Arlo's `ai-copywriter` contributes fact preservation and natural editing; do not mutate names, numbers or quotes to make text sound smoother.

Use concrete benefits supported by evidence, audience language and a clear next step. State what a claim rests on. Brand tone may vary between a hero, form error and pricing explanation while remaining recognizable. Improve awkward or generic phrasing without adding imaginary human experience. Do not use AI-detector scores or a fictional expert panel as a quality gate.

When editing approved copy, preserve intent and facts, distinguish necessary corrections from preference, and make significant proposition changes visible for review. Positioning changes belong in the optional positioning workflow, not a silent copy rewrite.
