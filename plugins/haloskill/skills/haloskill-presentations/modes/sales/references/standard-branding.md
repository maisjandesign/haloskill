## Phases

| Phase | Activities | Deliverable and decision | Source duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Brand background, mission, audience, market position, stakeholder interviews; competitors' websites, social activity and ads; visual moodboard | Research and moodboard in Figma; agree on the creative direction | 3 days |
| Developing Brand Identity | Concepts and rationale; identity previews on packaging, websites and social media | Brand Identity Concepts Presentation; 1–2 directions for client selection | 4 days |
| Defining Logo | Sketches, exploration, logo concepts, feedback and refinement | Logo drafts and concept explanations; 1–2 concepts for review and selection | 3–4 days |
| Brand Guidelines | Document identity standards and applications | Completed guideline presentation and Figma-based Brand Kit | 15–20 days |

## Guidelines coverage

- Logo: introduction, horizontal/vertical/minimal variants, construction, safe zones, usage rules and icon usage.
- Color: brand colors, shades, usage proportions and logo color combinations.
- Typography: main and additional fonts, styles and purchase links.
- Composition: logo placement, grid setup and layout composition.
- Elements: photography or illustration examples; pattern where relevant; icon style examples and a set purchase link.
- Brand in use: Figma cover templates for Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn; posters, billboard and merchandise mockups.

Final handoff (pp. 7–8): current logo assets, complete guidelines and a centralized Figma Brand Kit with logo, palette, typography and imagery.
The source lists PNG, SVG, PDF and AI logo formats. Confirm the production/export path before promising AI delivery; do not claim Figma exports native Illustrator files automatically.

## Feedback

- Two feedback rounds, allocatable across phases.
- 4–6 business days allocated to processing feedback.
- Small corrections taking less than one hour do not count as a full round; up to five per project.
- Major revisions requiring redesign count as one full round.
- Client feedback is due within 24 hours after an update from Halo Lab.

Source pp. 8–9. These are source package terms, not newly negotiated terms.
