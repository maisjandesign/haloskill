# Halo visual system

Visual source: https://halolab-design.webflow.io/#1
Logo master: ../assets/halo-logo.svg (the user's provided logo.svg).
Use the approved dark roadmap direction, not unrelated styling from a newer marketing homepage.

## Canvas and header

- Background: #02021E.
- Primary text: #F5F5F5. Secondary text: #A6A6B8.
- Purple active phase or outcome: #3719CA.
- Yellow timing, step or attention accent: #FDC448.
- Secondary periwinkle when needed: #5D7AFB.
- Hairlines: #4B4B64, approximately 1 px at 1920 × 1080.
- Safe margins: 80 px left and right.
- Header: official logo at x80/y40, 216 × 49.6; short client/deck label centered; page fraction at right; optional outlined proposal/status label.
- Header divider: y112. Content starts at y153 or lower.
- Keep the header and logo on every slide. Footer metadata, when used, sits below a line near y992.

## Type and scale

Use Suisse / Suisse Intl if installed and available. Otherwise use Arial, then Inter. Verify wrapping after any font fallback.

At 1920 × 1080:
- Main headline: typically 64–88 px, regular or medium, line height 1.05–1.15.
- Section or phase title: 32–44 px.
- Body: 24–30 px, line height 1.3–1.45. Short supporting labels can use 20–22 px.
- Header, eyebrow and footer: 16–19 px.
- Shorten or split dense content before shrinking body text.

## Composition families

1. Cover/opportunity: oversized title at left, purposeful brand or project visual at right, minimal supporting copy.
2. Roadmap: folder-tab phases with numbers, durations and deliverables. Highlight one phase in purple; leave others dark and outlined.
3. Scope detail: claim at left, one expanded detail area in the middle, a dominant deliverable or verified example at right.
4. Brand system: editorial category grid with hairlines and a single purple handoff band.
5. Investment/collaboration: purple investment folder at left, team or scope in the middle, feedback rules or next action at right.

Keep text editable and layouts native. Use actual supplied project imagery when relevant. SVG logos and icons must remain faithful to their source artwork.

Avoid decorative stock imagery, generic SaaS pricing cards, shadows, glass, neon glows, unrelated gradients and overcrowding. Do not reuse one composition mechanically across a long deck.
