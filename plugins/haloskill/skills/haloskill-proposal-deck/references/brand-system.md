# Halo Lab proposal brand system

## Authority

Primary reference: https://halolab-design.webflow.io/#1

This file translates the approved live presentation into reusable proposal rules. Match its visual grammar closely, while adapting the content to a client decision. Do not blend in the current site's white/serif direction unless the user explicitly requests it.

## Canvas and palette

- Format: 16:9 widescreen, 13.333 × 7.5 in.
- Background / ink field: `#02021E`.
- Primary text: `#F5F5F5`.
- Purple action/outcome: `#3719CA`.
- Yellow functional accent: `#FDC448`.
- Periwinkle secondary accent: `#5D7AFB`.
- Muted copy: use white at 58–72% opacity.
- Hairlines: white at 35–65% opacity, 0.5–0.75 pt.

Yellow communicates icons, status, or a precise point of attention. Purple communicates the active phase, output, or commercial emphasis. Neither color is decorative confetti.

## Typography

- Preferred family: Suisse / Suisse Intl when legally installed on the machine.
- Portable fallback: Arial, then Aptos. Never substitute a serif.
- Cover title: 56–64 pt, uppercase, tight line spacing, medium/bold.
- Interior claim/title: 30–36 pt, uppercase or compact title case.
- Step eyebrow and metadata: 8–10 pt, uppercase, tracked.
- Accordion title: 16–18 pt, medium.
- Body: 16–18 pt for client-facing readability; use 14 pt only for short legal or pricing notes.
- Page metadata: 8–10 pt.
- Use short lines and generous negative space. Avoid paragraphs longer than 45–55 words.

## Grid and geometry

- Outer safe margin: 0.4 in left/right and 0.35–0.45 in top/bottom.
- Interior header height: about 0.72 in including divider.
- Header: logo left, short deck/client label centered, page fraction plus outlined status/CTA right.
- Interior content starts at about 1.2 in from the top.
- Corner radius: 0.18–0.22 in for media and accordion containers.
- Use circles for step numbers and small icons.
- No shadows. Separation comes from outline, fill, scale, and negative space.

## Layout families

### 1. Minimal cover

- Left 42–48%: one oversized proposal title, optional client and date.
- Right 48–54%: one rounded visual window or a tightly art-directed collage.
- Bottom edge: website/contact blocks with small yellow icon tiles.
- No persistent interior header on the cover.

### 2. Roadmap / agenda

- Use a 2×3, 2×4, or 1×4 set of folder-tab tiles.
- Each tile has a circular step number and a concise phase name.
- Fill only the active/featured tile purple; keep the rest dark with thin white outlines.
- This pattern also works for options, workstreams, or deliverable groups.

### 3. Step / scope detail

- Left column, roughly 24%: eyebrow, decisive headline, and a purple folder/outcome block at the bottom.
- Middle column, roughly 29%: one expanded rounded accordion item and 2–4 collapsed items.
- Right column, roughly 43%: one dominant visual, diagram, case-study proof, or client artifact.
- The purple folder block answers “what the client gets” and “how long / how much,” not both unless the copy remains very short.

### 4. Proof / case study

- Keep the same three-zone grammar.
- Put the outcome claim at left, 2–3 evidence statements in the middle, and one strong project image or before/after at right.
- Use only verified figures. Put source/case-study URL in notes.

### 5. Investment / options

- Avoid generic pricing cards. Use folder-tab bands or a roadmap row.
- Make one recommended option purple and keep alternatives outlined.
- Show price, included scope, timing, and key exclusion in a fixed reading order.
- Never fabricate a “popular” badge or artificial discount.

### 6. Summary / next step

- Reuse the left claim + middle checklist structure.
- Right side may combine a dark media/proof panel with a white testimonial or contact card below.
- End on one explicit action with an owner or date when supplied.

## Imagery and diagrams

- Favor crisp editorial crops, interface fragments, identity applications, and real client materials.
- Keep one visual region dominant. Crop to fill its rounded window; never stretch.
- Use dark slate placeholder fills only while sourcing, and remove all placeholders before delivery unless clearly marked for user completion.
- Build simple native diagrams from editable shapes: nodes, arrows, labels, and lines. Do not rasterize diagrams.
- Available assets include the Halo logo, contact icons, check/info/analytics/image icons, a folder-tab shape, and three reference images. Reuse them selectively; they are not mandatory content.

## Anti-patterns

- White or beige canvas as the dominant background.
- Serif display typography.
- Purple gradients, neon glows, glass panels, drop shadows, or 3D chrome.
- Dense feature grids, dashboard-like layouts, or repeated generic cards.
- Tiny body text copied from the web reference without adapting it for slides.
- Decorative stock photos unrelated to the client or proposal.
- More than one expanded accordion item on the same slide.
