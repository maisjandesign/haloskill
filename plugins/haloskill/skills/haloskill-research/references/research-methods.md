# Research method selection

## Bounded investigation
Dean Peters `autonomous-investigation`: turn the brief into a decision and a few research questions, define a stopping rule, investigate, then synthesize. Do not force a menu or repeatedly ask permission for authorized reading. Use independent sources where possible and record missing evidence. Confidence depends on source quality, recency, applicability and corroboration.

## Competitor comparison
Dean Peters `competitive-research-snapshot` supplies the concise decision-focused comparison. Start with a manageable set of direct/indirect alternatives and explain selection. Compare audience, positioning, offer, capabilities, conversion path, credibility, strengths and gaps. Attribute claims to the competitor when not independently verified. Corey Haines `competitor-profiling` adds website/content/SEO depth when needed; unavailable paid tools mean narrower evidence, never fabricated traffic. Brand-building `competitor-branding` adds visual identity, tone and category conventions without pretending to know competitors' intentions.

## Voice of customer and journey
Dean Peters `voice-of-customer-miner` and Corey Haines `customer-research`: distinguish existing-client material, public evidence and primary research. Preserve quote, source URL/document location, date, audience context and sample limitations. Group needs, objections, switching triggers and vocabulary; do not convert every requested feature into a validated need. Interviews or user tests can validate uncertain themes. `jobs-to-be-done` separates functional/social/emotional jobs where supported. `customer-journey-map` follows the actual scenario, actions, touchpoints, friction and evidence rather than imposing a generic funnel.

## Industry evidence
Adapt the evidence discipline from lu90's industry research workflow: stable source IDs, exact source locations, collection date, numerical units/basis, explicit contradictions and a claim-to-source ledger. Do not import industry reports or assume their conclusions apply to this client. Deep industry research is conditional on the project's risk and unanswered questions.
